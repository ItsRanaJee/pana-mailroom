# Email rules for the Steep Club

Every newsletter issue must follow these five rules before it is sent.
An issue starts with a `Subject:` line and a `Preview:` line, then a blank
line, then the body.

1. **Subject.** The issue has a `Subject:` line. The subject is not empty and
   is 50 characters or fewer.
2. **Preview.** The issue has a `Preview:` line. The preview is not empty and
   is 90 characters or fewer.
3. **Greeting.** The body greets the reader by name with `{{first_name}}`.
4. **One link.** The body has exactly one link: one web address starting with
   `http://` or `https://`. Placeholders such as `{{unsubscribe_url}}` are not
   links.
5. **Footer.** The body ends with a footer that has the company address,
   `{{company_address}}`, and an unsubscribe link, `{{unsubscribe_url}}`.
